## What requires correction or qualification

### "Completely perpendicular planes"

This is a pedagogical picture. The Hodge dual is not fully defined by Euclidean perpendicularity. It depends on dimension, metric signature, orientation, and degree.

### "Electric transforms into negative magnetic and magnetic into electric"

This is convention-dependent shorthand. Exact signs depend on the field-tensor convention, metric signature, orientation, and definition of the observer-relative fields.

### `dF = 0` and `d*F = 4 pi J`

The compact form depends on unit system and on whether the current is represented as a one-form or its Hodge-dual three-form. SI, Gaussian, and natural-unit conventions differ.

### "Four pi appears because fields radiate like spheres"

Spherical flux geometry helps motivate `4 pi`, but the factor is fundamentally tied to unit conventions and normalization. It should not be used as a formal derivation.
