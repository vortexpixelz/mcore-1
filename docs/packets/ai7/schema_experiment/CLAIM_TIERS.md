# Claim Tiers

Use these tiers when filling schemas.

## Observed

Direct receipts, measurements, files, outputs, tests, or cited facts.

## Plausible

Reasonable interpretation from observed receipts, but not yet independently validated.

## Speculative

Useful hypothesis or direction, but not something to present as established.

## Forbidden for now

Interesting language or claim that should not be used externally until evidence improves.

## Rule

A strong schema makes it hard to accidentally move speculative material into observed status.
